# Templates

These templates help a beginner create complete machine-readable records. They
do not replace `PROJECT.json` or `registry/*.tsv`; completed answers must be
transferred into the corresponding registry rows and reviewed.

- `route-brief.md`: one candidate adaptation and its manuscript kernel;
- `execution-spike.md`: minimum data, code, figure, and overlap checks before
  a route can become P0;
- `route-mode-prompts.md`: prompts that prevent common adaptation modes from
  being omitted during candidate generation.
