# SPP1+TAM JITC Pilot

This directory audits one real anchor paper, generates bounded candidate
routes and records minimal real-data evidence. It is a Pilot, not a formal
manuscript project. Begin with `anchor/audit.md`; its detailed Figure framework
and flaw-to-repair contracts are in `anchor/framework-assets.md` and
`anchor/repair-contracts.md`. The reusable assets of nearby papers are separated
from their overlap risks in `anchor/donor-assets.md`; the open object, cell,
disease, evidence-order and validation combinations are tracked in
`anchor/combination-space.md`, while `anchor/route-rationale.md` records the
current AI judgment and its limits. Then use `paper2paper next .` to see evidence
gaps. Large data, credentials and copyrighted source PDFs stay outside Git.
